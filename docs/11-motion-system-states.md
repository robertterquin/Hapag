# Hapag Motion and System States

## Motion
- Use short 150–220ms transitions for chips, buttons, and cards.
- Use a gentle 300ms entrance for recipe results.
- Avoid excessive bouncing; motion should support orientation and delight.

## States
- Loading: skeleton cards and “Naghahanap ng ulam…” message.
- Empty: friendly pot illustration and a clear next action.
- Error: explain what happened and offer retry.
- Success: brief toast such as “Naisave na ang recipe!”
- AI uncertainty: show “Tantya lang ang presyo” or ask the user to confirm.
