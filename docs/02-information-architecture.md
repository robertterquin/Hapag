# Hapag Information Architecture

## Primary areas
- **Home** — ingredient input, quick filters, popular and recent recipes.
- **Ulam AI** — conversational ingredient-to-recipe assistant.
- **Recipe detail** — ingredients, cost, steps, substitutions, and cooking mode.
- **Saved** — favorites and cooked recipes.
- **Pantry** — recently used and tracked ingredients.

## Recipe categories
Breakfast, lunch, dinner, merienda, dessert, baon, budget meals, vegetarian, low-sodium, diabetic-friendly, allergy-aware, and regional Filipino dishes.

## Core entities
User, ingredient, pantry item, recipe, recipe suggestion, favorite, cooking session, dietary preference, and meal plan.
