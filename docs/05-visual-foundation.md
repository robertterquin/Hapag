# Hapag Visual Foundation

Hapag should feel like a cheerful digital Filipino kitchen: warm, colorful, rounded, and practical.

## Visual language
- Cream surfaces with colorful food-led accents.
- Rounded cards and pill-shaped ingredient chips.
- Friendly illustrations of a pot, plate, rice, sili, egg, spoon, and calamansi.
- Food photography should feel natural and home-cooked rather than overly polished.
- Use subtle banig, checkered tablecloth, or market-label patterns sparingly.

## Brand personality
Warm, playful, resourceful, encouraging, and proudly Filipino.
