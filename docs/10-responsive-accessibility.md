# Hapag Responsive and Accessibility Rules

## Responsive behavior
- Mobile first at 320px and up.
- One-column content on mobile; two- or three-column recipe grids on larger screens.
- Keep primary actions reachable near the bottom on mobile.
- Recipe steps remain readable without horizontal scrolling.

## Accessibility
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus rings.
- Semantic headings, labels, buttons, and landmarks.
- Minimum 44px touch targets.
- Do not rely on color alone for status or dietary warnings.
- Alt text for food imagery and decorative images marked appropriately.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
