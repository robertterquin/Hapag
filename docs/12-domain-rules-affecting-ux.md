# Domain Rules Affecting UX

- Prices are estimates and may vary by location, season, and store.
- Display prices in Philippine pesos using `₱`.
- Use Filipino measurements and terms where natural: kutsara, kutsarita, tasa, pakurot, gisa, sangkutsa, and ihawin.
- Support Tagalog, Taglish, and English input without requiring a language toggle.
- Treat allergies as safety-critical: never suggest a known allergen and show a warning when uncertainty exists.
- Dietary labels are guidance, not medical advice.
- Substitutions must preserve cooking method where possible and explain texture or taste changes.
- Never imply that AI-generated recipes have been professionally tested.
- Keep regional dish names and ingredient variants searchable.
